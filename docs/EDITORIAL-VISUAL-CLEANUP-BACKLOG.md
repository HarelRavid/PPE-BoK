# PPE-BoK — Final Editorial & Visual Cleanup Backlog

**Purpose:** Deferred book-wide cleanup after substantive content review is complete.

## Controlled reminder

During the chapter-by-chapter content review, substantive engineering content takes priority. Visual/editorial normalization is intentionally deferred so it does not interrupt technical closure.

After Chapters 000–012 complete substantive review, perform a dedicated final pass covering at minimum:

- punctuation placement and consistency;
- spacing around punctuation, symbols, units, dashes, parentheses, colons and semicolons;
- Hebrew/English directionality issues where applicable;
- heading hierarchy and numbering consistency;
- list indentation and bullet consistency;
- table alignment and column presentation;
- figure/table/equation label placement and visual consistency;
- blockquote, code, callout and emphasis formatting;
- line breaks and paragraph spacing;
- cross-reference presentation;
- typography consistency for units, variables, acronyms and technical symbols;
- final visual artwork replacing conceptual figure placeholders;
- global editorial polish for repeated wording and presentation-only inconsistencies.

## Rule

Do not allow these deferred presentation edits to reopen approved technical content unless the cleanup reveals a genuine technical ambiguity or content error. Any such issue becomes a new review finding and follows normal change control.
