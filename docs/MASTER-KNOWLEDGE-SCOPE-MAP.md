# PPE-BoK Master Knowledge-Scope Map

**Status:** ACTIVE RECOVERY / DO NOT FREEZE FINAL TOC  
**Purpose:** Preserve the maximum historically intended PPE-BoK knowledge scope independently of historical chapter count or numbering.  
**Governing rule:** A topic is not considered recovered merely because it appears briefly in a current chapter. Historical depth intent must be preserved.

---

## 1. Why this map exists

The PPE-BoK has passed through multiple architectures, including an early 20-chapter polymer-science concept, a much larger historical program extending at least through Chapter 78 and apparently toward approximately 91 chapters, and the current redevelopment architecture (Chapters 000–013).

The final book architecture shall therefore be **topic-driven, not chapter-count-driven**.

Every historically intended topic shall be assigned one of the following states before the final TOC is frozen:

- **FULLY COVERED** — current treatment preserves both subject and intended depth.
- **PARTIALLY COVERED** — current book includes the subject but not the intended depth or practical method.
- **ABSORBED / DUPLICATED** — historical topic is intentionally covered elsewhere with equivalent or better depth.
- **PLANNED BUT MISSING** — historically intended topic is not currently developed.
- **NEEDS DEEPER TREATMENT** — present only as summary/background but historically intended as a dedicated technical treatment.
- **HISTORICAL INTENT NOT YET VERIFIED** — suspected from numbering or context but exact topic evidence has not yet been recovered.

No final chapter count is to be selected until this map is complete.

---

## 2. Historical scope floor — ten project domains

The early master-course framework defined the PPE-BoK as covering the full plastic-piping field through ten domains:

1. Plastic Piping Fundamentals
2. Materials Science
3. International Standards
4. Engineering Design
5. Joining & Welding Technologies
6. Equipment & Process Systems
7. Quality, Inspection & Qualification
8. Failure Analysis
9. Industrial Case Studies
10. Future Technologies

These ten domains are treated as a minimum scope floor. A future architecture may distribute them across volumes or parts, but none may disappear accidentally.
