# PPE-BoK Engineering Doctrine

**Baseline:** 1.0

## Mission

PPE-BoK exists to transform engineering knowledge into engineering capability for plastic piping and pressure equipment.

Its purpose is not to replace engineering judgement, standards, codes or project specifications. Its purpose is to strengthen engineering judgement through structured knowledge, standards awareness, engineering reasoning and practical application.

## Core equation

**Design Basis + Engineering Principles + Standards Framework + Engineering Analysis + Engineering Judgement = Defensible Engineering Decision**

## Primary principles

1. **The chapter is the primary independent knowledge unit.** Investigations build progressively inside it.
2. **Engineering decisions are the objective.** Descriptive knowledge is included when it improves understanding, application, verification or risk reduction.
3. **Design Basis first.** Recommendations shall identify the operating context, assumptions, boundaries and governing requirements that make them valid.
4. **Standards first, but not standards alone.** Applicable standards are the authoritative technical framework. Scientific literature, handbooks and validated industry practice supplement them where appropriate.
5. **Explain the reason before the rule; apply the rule before concluding.** Understanding without application is incomplete; application without understanding is unsafe.
6. **Approximate 60/40 balance.** Aim for about 60% engineering application and 40% engineering explanation, but never force the ratio when the subject requires another balance.
7. **Engineering value over volume.** Every section should improve the reader's ability to understand, calculate, verify, specify, inspect, troubleshoot, prevent failure or make a better engineering decision.
8. **No engineering orphans.** Every equation serves a decision, every table answers a question, every figure communicates an engineering message, every example teaches a method, and every standards reference has engineering context.
9. **Context is mandatory.** No recommendation exists in isolation. State scope, assumptions, limits and applicable standards where relevant.
10. **Traceability.** Material technical statements shall be traceable to authoritative evidence. Generated text is never evidence.
11. **Engineering humility.** Uncertainty, engineering judgement, conflicting standards and knowledge gaps shall be visible rather than hidden behind artificial certainty.
12. **Standards validation before publication.** After authoring is complete, every standards-derived statement is rechecked against the authoritative source and applicable edition.

## Engineering value test

Content belongs in PPE-BoK when it materially helps the engineer to do at least one of the following:

- understand a governing mechanism;
- perform or verify a calculation;
- navigate or apply a standard;
- make or document a design decision;
- evaluate a material, component or system;
- inspect, test or accept engineering work;
- identify assumptions and limitations;
- reduce engineering risk;
- investigate or prevent failure;
- manage lifecycle decisions.

## Non-goals

PPE-BoK does not aim to:

- replace international or national standards;
- reproduce complete codes or copyrighted standards text;
- prescribe project-specific decisions without a Design Basis;
- become a manufacturer catalogue;
- include every available equation or polymer simply for completeness;
- use equations, tables, figures or case studies as decoration;
- present AI-generated output, common practice or manufacturer guidance as authoritative evidence.

## Standards neutrality

Where standards differ by scope, geography or methodology, PPE-BoK explains those differences and their engineering implications without declaring one universally superior unless authoritative evidence supports that conclusion.

## The desk test

A chapter is incomplete if a practicing engineer would not find it useful during real engineering work such as design, specification, procurement, review, inspection, commissioning, troubleshooting or failure investigation.

## The PPE-BoK promise

**Every chapter shall leave the reader better equipped to solve real engineering problems than before reading it.**
