# PPE-BoK Quality & Validation Manual

**Baseline:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This manual defines the review and validation gates required before a PPE-BoK chapter may enter publication baseline.

## 2. Quality gates

### Gate 1 — Technical Completeness
Confirm that the approved Chapter Design Brief has been satisfied and that required topics, calculations, standards paths, examples, tables, figures/placeholders and decision tools are present where applicable.

### Gate 2 — Engineering Quality
Review:
- correctness of mechanisms and reasoning;
- engineering usefulness and density;
- explicit assumptions and limitations;
- system-level consequences;
- decision logic;
- avoidance of unnecessary repetition;
- practical value to a working engineer.

### Gate 3 — Standards Validation
This gate occurs **after authoring is complete**.

Every standards-derived statement shall be rechecked against the authoritative source, not against memory, secondary literature, AI output or an earlier draft.

Verify, where applicable:
- standard number and title;
- edition / year;
- scope and applicability;
- clause / subclause;
- table / annex / figure / equation reference;
- terminology and symbols;
- numerical values and units;
- mandatory versus informative wording;
- exceptions, notes and boundary conditions;
- whether a statement is actually supported by the cited standard.

If the book and the authoritative standard conflict, the standard prevails and the book shall be corrected.

### Gate 4 — Equations, Units and Numerical Validation
For every material calculation:
- independently recalculate;
- check units and dimensions;
- check order of magnitude;
- confirm input values and source;
- confirm validity range;
- identify common misuse where relevant.

### Gate 5 — Evidence & Objectivity Review
Confirm:
- claims are traceable to the evidence hierarchy;
- facts are separated from engineering interpretation;
- manufacturer guidance is labelled as such;
- uncertainty and knowledge gaps are visible;
- project-specific examples are not presented as universal rules.

### Gate 6 — Engineering Consistency
Verify chapter-to-book consistency for:
- terminology;
- symbols;
- units;
- equation / table / figure numbering;
- status vocabulary;
- standards naming;
- cross-references;
- material classifications.

### Gate 7 — Reference Value / Desk Test
Ask:
**Would a practicing engineer keep this chapter open while performing real work?**

The engineer should be able to locate the relevant standards path, inputs, equations/method, limitations, examples and decision criteria without reading the whole book sequentially.

### Gate 8 — Editorial & Publication Approval
Language, navigation, captions and presentation are reviewed only after technical content and standards validation are complete.

## 3. Evidence hierarchy

Preferred authority order:
1. applicable legislation / regulation where relevant;
2. international / national standards and codes;
3. fundamental engineering science and authoritative handbooks/textbooks;
4. peer-reviewed literature;
5. professional organisation guidance;
6. validated manufacturer technical documentation;
7. documented industrial practice;
8. clearly labelled engineering inference.

Critical claims should, where reasonably possible, be cross-checked against more than one independent source. Standards-derived requirements still require direct verification against the standard itself.

## 4. Statement classification

Where needed, distinguish explicitly:
- Standard requirement
- Regulatory requirement
- Engineering principle
- Industry recommendation
- Manufacturer guidance
- Empirical correlation
- Mechanistic model
- Engineering inference
- Knowledge gap

## 5. Review status

Recommended chapter maturity:
- Level 0 — Draft
- Level 1 — Narrative / technical content complete
- Level 2 — Engineering assets integrated
- Level 3 — Standards integrated
- Level 4 — Technically reviewed
- Level 5 — Standards validated
- Level 6 — Publication ready

## 6. Change control

Changes are classified as:
- **A — Editorial:** no engineering meaning changed.
- **B — Structural:** chapter architecture / navigation change.
- **C — Technical:** engineering content, calculation, limit or recommendation changes; technical review required.
- **D — PDS / Doctrine:** changes governing methodology; explicit controlled approval required.

## 7. Final publication rule

**Write first. Verify before publication. Publish only after technical and standards validation.**
