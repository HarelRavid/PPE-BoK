# EDR-013 — Engineering Design Review

**Chapter:** 013 — Polyethylene (PE)  
**PDS Baseline:** 1.0  
**Reviewed source:** `chapter-013-redevelopment` draft  
**Status:** Approved Review Basis

## 1. Executive Summary

**Review decision:** CONDITIONAL PASS — ENGINEERING AUGMENTATION REQUIRED

Chapter 13 has a strong engineering architecture, sound technical reasoning and good explanatory quality. The review found no justification for wholesale rewriting. The principal deficiency is incomplete Engineering Reference content: key equations, tables, workflows, worked examples, standards navigation and chapter-end decision tools are not yet fully developed.

Recommended redevelopment mode: **Engineering Augmentation**, preserving strong narrative and reasoning while adding the engineering assets required by CDB-013 and PDS 1.0.

## 4. Pass 2A — Engineering Review, Investigations 1–4

### Investigation 1 — PASS with minor augmentation

Strengths: system-level framing, Design Basis orientation and balanced treatment of flexibility as both benefit and design obligation.

**GAP-013-E01 — Medium:** add a compact PE candidate-screening table linking characteristic → potential benefit → design obligation.

### Investigation 2 — STRONG PASS

The molecular / morphological depth is appropriate for an engineering handbook. No equation should be added merely to satisfy a quota. Retain the structure and ensure the planned visual maps structure → property → engineering consequence.

### Investigation 3 — CONDITIONAL PASS

The distinction between material classification and project approval is strong and should be preserved.

**GAP-013-E02 — High:** develop the classification / terminology reference table.  
**GAP-013-E03 — High:** complete PE100-RC treatment only after authoritative Standards Validation; final text must state standards status, qualification route and limits of inference.

### Investigation 4 — GOOD ENGINEERING, OVERLOADED

The thin-wall hoop-stress equation is correctly used for intuition rather than as the governing pressure-rating formula. The separation of creep, rupture and slow crack growth is strong, as is the distinction between nominal stress and local crack-driving condition.

**GAP-013-E04 — High:** complete Eq. 13-01 documentation with explicit source / derivation basis.  
**GAP-013-E05 — Medium:** relocate detailed failure-evidence discussion to Investigation 10.  
**GAP-013-E06 — Medium:** connect SCG discussion more explicitly to qualification / testing pathways.

## 5. Pass 2B — Core Engineering Assessment, Investigations 5–10

Investigations 5–10 are currently incomplete and constitute the majority of the missing Engineering Reference layer.

### Investigation 5 — Long-term testing

Required role: explain the evidence chain, not reproduce a test-standard procedure.

`Controlled specimens → pressure / temperature test evidence → time-to-failure data → regression → classification input`

### Investigation 6 — Regression

Required role: explain how the practicing engineer interprets the regression output and lower-confidence behaviour; do not turn the chapter into a statistics tutorial.

### Investigation 7 — MRS / design stress

This is a core engineering section and requires equations, definitions, units, standards attribution, validity limits, decision flow and practical interpretation.

### Investigation 8 — SDR / pressure designation / marking

This should function as a mini-reference section: numbered equations, worked example, SDR / pressure relationship table, pipe-marking interpretation and explicit limitations.

### Investigation 9 — Project Design Basis integration

This should be a controlled engineering workflow, not a list of “also check” items.

### Investigation 10 — Failure Lens

Retain the concept of failure thinking rather than full failure-analysis methodology. Add a structured evidence / investigation workflow and failure-mechanism / evidence / response table.

## 7. Final Recommendation

Do **not** perform wholesale rewrite.

Preserve the strong explanatory and engineering-reasoning material. Execute controlled Engineering Augmentation through the approved RDP, focusing on:

- standards navigation;
- numbered equations with complete equation metadata;
- classification and design tables;
- worked examples;
- engineering workflows;
- pipe-marking interpretation;
- Design Review Checklist;
- chapter closure and cross-references;
- final Standards Validation.

## 8. Acceptance Basis

Chapter 13 may advance to final approval only when CDB-013 success criteria are met, all EDR gaps are closed or formally dispositioned, planned examples are independently recalculated, Technical Review is passed and Standards Validation is completed against authoritative sources.

## 9. Candidate Lessons Learned

The following remain candidate lessons during the frozen PDS 1.0 pilot:

- EDR before redevelopment can prevent unnecessary rewriting.
- Strong engineering narrative may require augmentation rather than replacement.
- Gap Registers and Work Packages improve traceability.
- A Desk Test may be useful as a future PDS 1.1 review criterion.

These are not active PDS requirements until post-pilot change control is completed.
