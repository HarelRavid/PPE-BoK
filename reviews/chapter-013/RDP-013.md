## 3. Execution Sequence

Execute WP-013-01 through WP-013-10 as enabling / scope-control work, then develop the core chain in order: WP-013-11 → 12 → 13 → 14 → 15 → 16. Complete chapter closure only after the engineering assets are stable.

## 4. Engineering Assets Required

Use approved IDs such as `EQ-013-001`, `FIG-013-001`, `TAB-013-001`, `WF-013-001`, `EX-013-001`, `CL-013-001`.

Minimum planned assets are those already required by CDB-013: classification table, MRS/coefficient/SDR/pressure reference table, design-input checklist, pipe-marking table, failure table, two worked examples, full PE engineering workflow, chapter-end checklist and original figure placeholders.

## 5. Standards Validation Hold Points

Re-open authoritative sources after authoring and verify at minimum: standard editions, scope, clause/table references, MRS terminology and example values, design coefficients/factors, pressure relationship notation, temperature treatment, PE100-RC status, product marking requirements and any normative wording.

## 6. Completion Gate

RDP-013 closes only when all linked gaps are closed or formally dispositioned, Technical Review passes, both worked examples are independently recalculated, and final Standards Validation is complete.
