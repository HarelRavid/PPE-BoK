# PPE-BoK Engineering Reviews

This directory contains controlled engineering-review evidence and reusable review templates.

## Structure

- `templates/` — active PDS 1.0 review templates for EDR, RDP, Gap Registers, Review Logs and final review checklists.
- `chapter-NNN/` — chapter-specific formal review packages where a chapter requires an EDR/RDP or other controlled review record.

## Chapter 013

`chapter-013/` is the historical formal review package created during the Chapter 13 PDS pilot. These records are retained as evidence of the pilot review decisions.

They shall not be rewritten merely to make them look current with later branch work. If Chapter 13 redevelopment closes additional gaps, the closure evidence should be recorded through the controlled Chapter 13 workflow rather than silently overwriting the historical review record.

## Review hierarchy

- The approved CDB defines chapter-specific scope and acceptance criteria.
- PDS 1.0 defines the review/validation method.
- Review templates provide a consistent record structure.
- A review record is evidence, not a replacement for the chapter or authoritative standards.
- Technical Review PASS does not replace final Standards Validation or the other gates in `governance/Definition-of-Done.md`.

Archived/superseded review material, if any, belongs under `archive/` and is non-governing.
