# Chapter 015 — Investigation 8 Primary-Evidence Gate

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Investigation:** 8 — What Changes with Homogeneous / Metallocene Coordination Polymerization?  
**Gate:** metallocene / `single-site` architecture claims  
**Disposition:** **PASS FOR BOUNDED AUTHORING — TWO CONTROLLED PRIMARY CASES**

## 1. Terminology layer

IUPAC Gold Book / S015-004 establishes:

- `09005` — homogeneous catalysis coordination polymerization uses a homogeneous catalyst;
- metallocene polymerization is one form of homogeneous coordination polymerization;
- `09006` — metallocene polymerization is coordination polymerization making use of a metallocene catalyst.

These definitions do **not** state that every metallocene system is single-site in an operational sense, nor that it must produce a narrow MWD, uniform comonomer distribution or one stereoregularity.

## 2. Controlled primary sources

### S015-012 — Kenyon et al. (2022)

**Citation:** Philip Kenyon, D. W. Justin Leung, Zoë R. Turner, Jean-Charles Buffet, Dermot O’Hare, _Tuning Polyethylene Molecular Weight Distributions Using Catalyst Support Composition_, Macromolecules 55(9) (2022), 3408–3414. DOI `10.1021/acs.macromol.1c02604`.

**Access / review:** open-access ACS article text/abstract and Oxford repository metadata directly reviewed.

**System:** rac-ethylenebis(1-indenyl)zirconium dichloride [(EBI)ZrCl2] immobilized on tunable nickel-containing layered-double-hydroxide-derived supports for ethylene polymerization.

**Supported use:**

- a single immobilized metallocene catalyst precursor on one support under one stated reaction condition set produced bimodal polyethylene in the reported system;
- molecular-weight distribution changed with support composition and reaction conditions;
- support/immobilization/process environment can therefore break the simplistic expectation that a metallocene label alone guarantees a narrow monomodal MWD.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- all supported metallocenes produce broad/bimodal MWD;
- all homogeneous metallocenes cease to behave as defined sites;
- the reported support mechanism applies to commercial pipe resins;
- any direct piping-performance conclusion.

### S015-013 — De Rosa et al. (2004)

**Citation:** Claudio De Rosa, Finizia Auriemma, Annunziata Di Capua, Luigi Resconi, Simona Guidotti, Isabella Camurati, Ilya E. Nifant’ev, Ilya P. Laishevtsev, _Structure–Property Correlations in Polypropylene from Metallocene Catalysts: Stereodefective, Regioregular Isotactic Polypropylene_, Journal of the American Chemical Society 126(51) (2004), 17040–17049. DOI `10.1021/ja045684f`.

**Access / review:** ACS primary-paper abstract and metadata directly reviewed.

**System:** series of MAO-activated C1-symmetric zirconocene catalysts producing isotactic polypropylene.

**Supported use:**

- the studied zirconocene series produced polypropylenes with different degrees of stereoregularity;
- metallocene/ligand/catalyst identity is therefore part of the causal provenance and the word `metallocene` does not specify one universal stereoregularity outcome.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- all metallocenes permit the same stereochemical control;
- any particular ligand architecture guarantees a piping property;
- the studied PP results apply directly to PE or to commercial pipe compounds.

## 3. Controlled authoring synthesis

Investigation 8 may state:

1. metallocene polymerization is one form of homogeneous coordination polymerization, not a synonym for all homogeneous coordination polymerization;
2. `single-site` may be useful descriptive/industrial language for well-defined catalyst environments, but it is not sufficient by itself as a measured architecture specification;
3. narrow MWD / uniform composition are **possible outcomes in defined systems**, not definition-level consequences of `metallocene`;
4. support, activation, ion-pair environment, catalyst identity and reaction conditions can alter the polymer output even when one metallocene precursor is used;
5. metallocene catalyst design can alter stereoregularity in defined PP systems;
6. all architecture claims must ultimately be verified by polymer characterization.

## 4. Prohibited synthesis

Do not author:

- `metallocene = narrow MWD` as a universal rule;
- `metallocene = uniform comonomer distribution` as a universal rule;
- `homogeneous = single-site` as a definition;
- `single-site = one molecular weight`;
- `metallocene is superior to Ziegler–Natta`;
- direct metallocene → pipe performance claims.

## 5. Gate decision

**PASS FOR INVESTIGATION 8 CONTROLLED AUTHORING.**

Authorized named primary cases: S015-012 and S015-013 only.
