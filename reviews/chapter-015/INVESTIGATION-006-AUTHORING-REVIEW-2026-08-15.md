## 2. Technical review

PASS.

The candidate correctly:

- places coordination polymerization inside the chain-polymerization branch;
- defines it through preliminary coordination of monomer with the chain carrier;
- distinguishes `coordination polymerization` from `coordination polymer`;
- distinguishes catalyst precursor/precatalyst from the active catalyst produced through initialization;
- uses coordination-insertion polymerization specifically for olefin insertion into a metal–carbon bond;
- preserves IUPAC's deprecation of unexplained `metal-catalyzed polymerization` as a substitute for coordination-polymerization mechanism;
- distinguishes heterogeneous/homogeneous **catalyst** classification from the phase state of the reaction medium;
- does not assume heterogeneous catalyst = known active-site distribution or homogeneous catalyst = metallocene.
