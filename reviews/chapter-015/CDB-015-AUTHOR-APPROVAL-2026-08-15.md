## Controlled boundaries retained

Approval does not waive the CDB evidence rules. In particular:

- formal terminology shall distinguish chain polymerization, polyaddition and polycondensation rather than relying on the older `addition polymerization / condensation polymerization` classroom split;
- `Ziegler–Natta` may be used as established industrial/historical terminology only with the modern coordination-polymerization terminology made explicit;
- catalyst/process information may generate architecture hypotheses but may not be converted directly into commercial-grade or piping-performance conclusions;
- named material/catalyst/process effects require directly reviewed primary evidence and transferability controls;
- detailed molecular architecture remains owned by Chapter 016, morphology by Chapter 017, thermal response by Chapter 018, viscoelasticity by Chapter 019, and fracture/degradation by Chapter 020.
