## 2. Technical review

PASS.

- maps additive step polymerization to polyaddition and condensative step polymerization to polycondensation;
- uses the IUPAC `P_x + P_y` growth notation to show that both reacting species may have degrees of polymerization greater than one;
- separates by-product character from the top-level step/chain mechanism classification;
- defines monomer functionality as possible covalent connectivity rather than guaranteed final topology;
- preserves the IUPAC distinction that `f = 2` permits linear-chain or macrocyclic structures, while `f > 2` can permit branch points/networks;
