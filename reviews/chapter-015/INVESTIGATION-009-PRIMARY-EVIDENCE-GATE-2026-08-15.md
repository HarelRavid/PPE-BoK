# Chapter 015 — Investigation 9 Primary-Evidence Gate

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Investigation:** 9 — How Do Process Variables Become Molecular-Architecture Hypotheses?  
**Gate:** named process-variable → measured material-output claims  
**Disposition:** **PASS FOR BOUNDED AUTHORING — FOUR CONTROLLED PRIMARY CASES**

## 1. Gate purpose

Investigation 9 is the chapter's main process→architecture evidence gate.

General polymerization mechanism supports only the proposition that process variables can alter competing reaction pathways. It does not support a universal directional rule such as:

- `more hydrogen → lower molecular weight`;
- `more comonomer → more/less branching`;
- `higher temperature → lower molecular weight`;
- `longer residence time → one fixed architecture change`;
- `supported catalyst → broad MWD`.

Any retained directional statement must be bounded to a directly reviewed primary system.

## 2. Controlled primary sources

### S015-014 — Czaja & Białek (2001): hydrogen in ethylene/alpha-olefin copolymerization

**Citation:** Krystyna Czaja and Marzena Białek, _Microstructure of ethylene-1-hexene and ethylene-1-octene copolymers obtained over Ziegler–Natta catalysts supported on MgCl2(THF)2_, Polymer 42(6) (2001), 2289–2297. DOI `10.1016/S0032-3861(00)00621-2`.

**System:** ethylene copolymerization with 1-hexene or 1-octene in the presence of hydrogen using three supported catalysts: `MgCl2(THF)2/VOCl3/Et2AlCl`, `MgCl2(THF)2/VCl4/Et2AlCl`, and `MgCl2(THF)2/TiCl4/Et2AlCl`.

**Directly reviewed evidence:** publisher primary-paper abstract/metadata.

**Supported Chapter 015 use:**

- in all three studied catalyst systems, adding hydrogen to the copolymerization feed reduced the molecular weight of the produced copolymers and reduced catalyst activity;
- 13C NMR was used to examine copolymer microstructure;
- the result makes hydrogen a legitimate system-specific chain-building/process variable.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- the same magnitude or direction for every coordination-polymerization catalyst;
- hydrogen always acts through one identical mechanistic path;
- the observed molecular-weight change directly predicts pipe performance;
- hydrogen concentration can be converted into a universal resin molecular-weight setpoint.

### S015-015 — Zhang et al. (2021): comonomer feed ratio and active-center/copolymer fractions

**Citation:** Biao Zhang, Qingyun Qian, Pengjia Yang, Baiyu Jiang, Zhisheng Fu, Zhiqiang Fan, _Responses of a Supported Ziegler–Natta Catalyst to Comonomer Feed Ratios in Ethylene–Propylene Copolymerization: Differentiation of Active Centers with Different Catalytic Features_, Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research 60(12) (2021), 4575–4588. DOI `10.1021/acs.iecr.1c00104`.

**System:** ethylene–propylene copolymerization with a `TiCl4/Di/MgCl2–TEA/De` Ziegler–Natta catalyst at varied ethylene/propylene feed ratios.

**Directly reviewed evidence:** ACS primary-paper abstract/metadata and listed Supporting Information scope.

**Supported Chapter 015 use:**

- the study deliberately varied E/P feed ratio and measured the distribution of active centers among fractionated copolymer populations;
- the reported active-center/copolymer-fraction distribution changed with comonomer feed ratio in the defined system;
- different active-center categories were associated with different copolymer fraction/microstructure behavior in the study;
- comonomer feed ratio is therefore a legitimate provenance variable that requires direct composition/microstructure evidence.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- one universal E/P feed-ratio → sequence-distribution relationship for all catalysts;
- direct transfer to PE/1-hexene pipe grades;
- feed ratio alone establishes final crystallinity/mechanical/piping performance.

### S015-016 — Wang et al. (1998): temperature, residence time and ethylene feed in continuous solution polymerization

**Citation:** Wen-Jun Wang, Dajing Yan, Shiping Zhu, Archie E. Hamielec, _Kinetics of Long Chain Branching in Continuous Solution Polymerization of Ethylene Using Constrained Geometry Metallocene_, Macromolecules 31(25) (1998), 8677–8683. DOI `10.1021/ma980914u`.

**System:** continuous solution ethylene polymerization in a high-temperature/high-pressure CSTR using the constrained-geometry catalyst system `[C5Me4(SiMe2NtBu)]TiMe2 / tris(pentafluorophenyl)boron / modified methylaluminoxane` in Isopar E; reported pressure 500 psig.

**Directly reviewed evidence:** ACS primary-paper abstract/metadata.

**Supported Chapter 015 use:**

- polymerization variables included temperature, mean residence time and ethylene feed concentration;
- the produced polyethylene samples exhibited measured long-chain-branching behavior in the studied system;
- the authors reported active-center decay with mean residence time and estimated rate parameters for propagation, LCB and transfer reactions;
- elevated-temperature samples showed methyl side-chain evidence interpreted in the context of the studied catalyst/mechanism;
- residence/reaction history and temperature are therefore legitimate architecture-hypothesis inputs in a defined continuous process.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- higher temperature universally creates more LCB in polyethylene;
- the reported kinetic constants transfer to other catalysts/reactors;
- a CSTR residence time is a direct piping-material specification;
- the studied LCB levels directly establish piping performance.

### S015-012 — Kenyon et al. (2022): support composition / reaction environment and MWD

**Citation:** Philip Kenyon, D. W. Justin Leung, Zoë R. Turner, Jean-Charles Buffet, Dermot O’Hare, _Tuning Polyethylene Molecular Weight Distributions Using Catalyst Support Composition_, Macromolecules 55(9) (2022), 3408–3414. DOI `10.1021/acs.macromol.1c02604`.

**System:** `(EBI)ZrCl2` immobilized on tunable Ni-containing layered-double-hydroxide-derived supports for ethylene polymerization.

**Directly reviewed evidence:** open-access ACS primary article / repository metadata.

**Supported Chapter 015 use:**

- support composition and reaction conditions altered the molecular-weight-distribution response in the defined supported-metallocene system;
- one immobilized metallocene precursor/support system could produce bimodal polyethylene under the reported conditions;
- support/immobilization environment is therefore part of process/catalyst provenance rather than an irrelevant implementation detail.

**Unsupported transfer:**

- every support change alters MWD;
- every supported metallocene gives bimodal PE;
- support composition alone determines pipe performance.

## 3. Controlled synthesis allowed in Investigation 9

Investigation 9 may state:

1. process variables are **causal/provenance inputs**, not product design values;
2. hydrogen can alter molecular-weight/activity outcomes in defined coordination-polymerization systems, but the magnitude/mechanism must be established for the actual system;
3. comonomer feed ratio can alter active-center utilization and copolymer composition/microstructure in a defined heterogeneous catalyst system;
4. temperature, mean residence time and feed concentration can participate in chain-building/LCB history in a defined continuous metallocene process;
5. support/immobilization/reaction environment can alter MWD even when the named metallocene precursor is unchanged;
6. every process→architecture hypothesis must identify the measurement that would test it.

## 4. Prohibited synthesis

Do not write:

- `hydrogen always lowers molecular weight` without system qualifier;
- `more comonomer always increases branching uniformly`;
- `higher temperature always produces more LCB`;
- `longer residence time always lowers activity/MW`;
- `supported catalyst means broad/bimodal MWD`;
- any process-variable → SCG/toughness/fusion/pressure/lifetime conclusion without downstream characterization and qualification.

## 5. Required evidence matrix

Investigation 9 shall include a matrix with at least:

`system | process variable | directly measured output | supported conclusion | confounders / coupled variables | unsupported conclusion | piping transferability`.

The matrix must make it impossible to read a source-specific trend as a universal design rule.

## 6. Gate decision

**PASS FOR INVESTIGATION 9 CONTROLLED AUTHORING.**

Authorized named cases: S015-014, S015-015, S015-016 and reuse of S015-012 only.

No additional named process/material case may be introduced without a new direct-source gate.
