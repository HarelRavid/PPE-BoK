## 1. Review basis

Terminology was checked against S015-004 / Gold Book entries `09005` and `09006`.

Named architecture claims are restricted to the two pre-approved primary systems:

- S015-012 — Kenyon et al. 2022, supported `(EBI)ZrCl2` ethylene polymerization / support-composition MWD study;
- S015-013 — De Rosa et al. 2004, MAO-activated C1-symmetric zirconocene polypropylene stereoregularity study.

## 2. Technical review

PASS.

The candidate correctly:

- treats metallocene polymerization as one form of homogeneous coordination polymerization rather than as a synonym for the entire homogeneous category;
- distinguishes homogeneous **catalyst** from homogeneous reaction medium;
- treats `single-site` as descriptive/industrial language requiring a defined meaning and supporting evidence;
- rejects `metallocene = narrow MWD` as a definition-level rule;
- rejects `metallocene = uniform comonomer distribution` as a definition-level rule;
- preserves support, activation and process history as possible modifiers of the effective catalytic environment;
- does not rank homogeneous/metallocene technology as inherently superior to heterogeneous/Ziegler–Natta technology.

## 3. Primary-evidence review

PASS within the controlled gate.

### S015-012

Used as a falsification case only: in the defined supported-metallocene ethylene system, one immobilized metallocene precursor/support configuration produced bimodal polyethylene and the MWD changed with support composition / reaction conditions. The manuscript explicitly rejects transfer to all supported or homogeneous metallocenes.

### S015-013

Used only to show that the studied zirconocene catalyst series produced polypropylene with different degrees of stereoregularity. The manuscript does not generalize that result to all metallocenes, to polyethylene or to piping performance.

No unsupported universal MWD, comonomer-distribution, stereoregularity or performance claim is retained.

PASS as the completed scientific specification across Investigations 6–8.

The final figure must preserve the central warning that heterogeneous/homogeneous/metallocene/Ziegler–Natta are catalyst/mechanism descriptors, not performance rankings.

### EX-015-001

## 5. Editorial / engineering-use review

PASS.

The Investigation uses the supported-metallocene primary case effectively as a falsification tool: even a defined catalyst precursor does not erase support/activation/process history. This reinforces the Chapter 015 provenance framework without duplicating Chapter 016 architecture treatment.

## 6. Gate decision

**INVESTIGATION 8 — PASS.**

Investigation 9 is now the main material/process primary-evidence gate. Before authoring any directional process-variable statement, build a controlled evidence matrix with direct primary sources for the exact system and variable.

Candidate variables include:

- hydrogen / chain transfer;
- monomer/comonomer feed;
- polymerization temperature;
- pressure / monomer-concentration context;
- residence/reaction history;
- support/activation/process configuration.

No generic rule such as `more hydrogen → lower MW`, `more comonomer → X branching`, or `higher temperature → lower MW` is authorized without direct system-specific evidence and confounder control.
