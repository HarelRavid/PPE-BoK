## 1. Review basis

Terminology was checked against Gold Book `09004` / S015-004.

Material/site claims are limited to the three pre-approved primary systems:

- S015-009 — Piovano et al. 2018, operando MgCl2-based catalyst genesis;
- S015-010 — Busico et al. 1999, high-resolution 13C NMR / site-model analysis for one MgCl2-supported PP catalyst system;
- S015-011 — Guo et al. 2025, defined MgCl2-supported PE/alpha-olefin catalyst active-species / branch-distribution study.

## 2. Technical review

PASS.

The candidate correctly:

- retains `Ziegler–Natta` as industrial/historical language while using heterogeneous catalysis coordination polymerization as the controlled category;
- distinguishes heterogeneous **catalyst** from heterogeneous reaction medium;
- rejects the universal shortcut `heterogeneous = multi-site = broad MWD`;
- treats site count/site identity as a measurement/model question;
- distinguishes catalyst/site evidence from polymer architecture evidence;
- keeps the evidence chain open through morphology, product qualification and application suitability.

## 3. Primary-evidence review

PASS within the controlled gate.

### S015-009
Used only to show complexity and sequential catalyst genesis/activation in the studied MgCl2/TiCl4/AlEt3 system.

### S015-010
Used only to show that, in the specified PP catalyst system, a three-site statistical model matched high-resolution stereosequence data better than tested two-site models. The manuscript explicitly rejects universalizing that result to every Ziegler–Natta catalyst.

### S015-011
Used only to show a defined-system correlation between catalyst-state populations and measured short-chain-branch distribution. The manuscript explicitly rejects transfer to all Ziegler–Natta PE or to piping performance.

No unsupported universal MWD, branching, stereoregularity or performance claim is retained.

## 4. Asset review

### FIG-015-004 heterogeneous branch
PASS. Mandatory warning `heterogeneous catalyst ≠ fixed active-site distribution ≠ fixed polymer architecture` is technically appropriate.

### Three-source evidence matrix
PASS. Supported/unsupported conclusions remain visible.

## 5. Editorial / engineering-use review

PASS.

The Investigation gives the engineer a practical way to interpret supplier/change-control language without demanding proprietary catalyst recipes. The recommended route is actual resin characterization + qualification evidence.

## 6. Gate decision

**INVESTIGATION 7 — PASS.**

Investigation 8 may now proceed only after a separate primary-evidence gate for any retained claims about:

- `single-site` behavior;
- narrow MWD;
- comonomer distribution;
- stereoregularity/selectivity;
- metallocene versus heterogeneous catalyst outcomes.

IUPAC terminology alone remains sufficient only for the statements that homogeneous catalysis coordination polymerization uses a homogeneous catalyst and that metallocene polymerization is one form of homogeneous coordination polymerization.
