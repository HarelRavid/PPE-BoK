## 3. Editorial findings for integration

### ED015-01 — normalize the post-2026 terminology update

The integrated manuscript, Quick Navigation, Technical Outline and CDB shall use the same current naming hierarchy and Investigation 5 title.

Do not leave a reader-facing contradiction in which one file says `step-growth` is only explanatory while the integrated Investigation treats `step polymerization` as the formal current term.

### ED015-02 — normalize molar-mass wording

Prefer `molar mass` in prose.

Where `MWD` is retained as the familiar abbreviation, define it once as `molar-mass distribution (molecular-weight distribution, MWD, in common/source usage)` or another concise controlled convention.

Do not alter original publication titles.

### ED015-03 — remove only redundant generic warnings

The chapter intentionally repeats evidence boundaries because each mechanism presents a different over-inference risk.

During integration, remove only literally redundant generic warnings that interrupt flow. Retain mechanism-specific cautions such as:

- chain vs step classification;
- termination vs dormancy;
- heterogeneous ≠ fixed site distribution;
- metallocene ≠ guaranteed narrow MWD;
- process variable ≠ universal directional rule.

### ED015-04 — asset ownership must be visually unambiguous

Final reader-facing text shall show one formal specification for each `FIG-015-001` through `FIG-015-006` and `TAB-015-001` through `TAB-015-005`.

Precursor discussion is acceptable, but duplicate final labels are not.

### ED015-05 — keep source-case boundaries close to the case

For S015-009 through S015-016, retain the pattern:

`what this supports → what this does not support / transferability`.

Do not move all limitations to a distant endnote; the boundary is part of the teaching value.

### ED015-06 — preserve industry vocabulary without allowing it to control taxonomy

`Ziegler–Natta`, `single-site`, `MWD` and `metallocene` are familiar and useful to the intended audience.

Use them, but place current controlled terminology immediately around them so the chapter remains practical and scientifically precise.
