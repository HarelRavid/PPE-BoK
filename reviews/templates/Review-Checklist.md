# Engineering Review Checklist

**Chapter:** XXX  
**PDS Baseline:** 1.0

Use this checklist as a final review aid, not as a substitute for the PDS, CDB, technical judgement or authoritative Standards Validation.

## Architecture
- [ ] Chapter mission and scope remain clear.
- [ ] Chapter matches the approved CDB.
- [ ] Investigations progress without unnecessary repetition.
- [ ] Chapter boundaries and controlled cross-references are respected.
- [ ] Working architecture references agree with `BOOK_STRUCTURE.md`.

## Engineering Content
- [ ] Governing mechanisms and reasoning have passed Technical Review.
- [ ] Required Design Basis inputs are identifiable.
- [ ] Equations used for engineering decisions include symbols, units, assumptions, validity limits and source/derivation basis.
- [ ] Important limitations and common misuses are explicit.
- [ ] Specialist-analysis boundaries are visible.

## Engineering Utility
- [ ] A practicing engineer can locate the main engineering method quickly.
- [ ] Required tables/workflows/checklists materially reduce engineering effort.
- [ ] Worked examples, where useful, follow the PDS example structure and state what they do not prove.
- [ ] Chapter closure states the engineering decision/capability achieved.

## Standards
- [ ] Standards navigation is specific enough to identify the governing path.
- [ ] Applicability dependencies are stated rather than hidden behind generic wording.
- [ ] Product/design/application/test standards are not conflated.
- [ ] Standards-derived claims, values, coefficients, editions and references have completed the final Standards Validation gate before publication approval.

## Evidence and Objectivity
- [ ] Material claims are traceable to appropriate evidence.
- [ ] Manufacturer guidance and industry practice are labelled correctly.
- [ ] Engineering inference is visible where used.
- [ ] Transferability limits are addressed for academic or scaled evidence.
- [ ] Knowledge gaps and uncertainty are not hidden.

## Assets and Navigation
- [ ] Engineering assets use approved chapter-based IDs.
- [ ] Figures/placeholders have a defined engineering purpose and source status.
- [ ] Cross-references and chapter closure are complete.
- [ ] The chapter-specific source register and central Standards Register are synchronized where required.

## Review Closure
- [ ] EDR gaps are closed or formally dispositioned where an EDR was required.
- [ ] RDP work packages are closed with evidence where an RDP was required.
- [ ] Technical Review passed.
- [ ] Independent calculation / example checks passed.
- [ ] Standards Validation passed where applicable.
- [ ] Evidence/objectivity review passed.
- [ ] Editorial/style review passed.
- [ ] Desk Test passed.
- [ ] Joint author review completed.
- [ ] Explicit author approval recorded.
- [ ] Approved revision is ready for repository baseline / merged to `main` as required.
