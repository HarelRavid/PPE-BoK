**Document ID:** RDP-XXX  
**Chapter:** XXX  
**Source Review:** EDR-XXX  
**PDS Baseline:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft / Approved / In Execution / Closed

> Use this plan to convert approved EDR findings into controlled work packages. The RDP implements the approved chapter disposition; it does not silently modify the PDS or final book architecture.

## 1. Objective

State the approved redevelopment objective and mode, for example Retain, Engineering Augmentation, Targeted Rewrite or Major Redevelopment.

## 2. Governing inputs

Identify:

- approved EDR;
- approved CDB;
- current chapter revision;
- applicable `BOOK_STRUCTURE.md` architecture constraints;
- relevant PDS/governance documents;
- review/standards hold points.

## 3. Constraints

- Preserve approved strong content unless a documented gap requires change.
- Work only from approved findings, the chapter CDB and controlled architecture requirements.
- Standards-derived claims remain subject to final authoritative Standards Validation.
- Technical meaning shall not be changed outside an approved work package.
- No work package is closed without evidence against its acceptance criteria.

## 8. Technical / Specialist Review Hold Points

## 9. Cross-book impacts

Record any controlled cross-reference, terminology, standards-register, glossary or architecture updates required by the redevelopment. Do not change final numbering merely to resolve a local chapter issue.

## 10. Completion Gate

The RDP is complete when every work package is either Closed with evidence or formally dispositioned, chapter acceptance criteria are satisfied, Technical Review is passed, required independent checks are complete and Standards Validation has been completed where required.

Closure of an RDP does not bypass the remaining gates in `governance/Definition-of-Done.md`.
