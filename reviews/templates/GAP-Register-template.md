## Severity Guidance

**Critical** — prevents technically defensible use or creates an unacceptable safety/compliance risk; redevelopment cannot close without resolution.

**High** — materially limits engineering correctness, utility, traceability or standards navigation.

**Medium** — meaningful deficiency that should be corrected but does not invalidate the core engineering approach.

**Low** — localized improvement with limited engineering consequence.

Severity describes engineering consequence, not writing effort.

## Closure Rule

A gap is Closed only when the linked change exists and the reviewer records evidence that the finding has been resolved. A gap may be formally accepted/deferred only with documented rationale and approval.
