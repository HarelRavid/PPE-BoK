**Investigation:** 5 — What Is Branching, and Why Is “More Branching” an Incomplete Statement?  
**Disposition:** **PASS**

## Review scope

Reviewed the controlled candidate against CDB-016, the Technical Outline and current IUPAC architecture terminology.

## Findings

- `branch`, `branch point`, `branched chain` and `branched polymer` are separated correctly.
- Structural polymer branching is explicitly distinguished from kinetic `chain branching` terminology.
- Branch-point count, branch length, branch-length distribution, placement, chemistry and topology are treated as separate variables.
- No universal numeric SCB/LCB threshold is invented.
- Branch point is not equated with crosslink.
- Branching is not equated with entanglement.
- Density is treated only as possible downstream/correlated evidence, not a unique branch-distribution measurement.
- IUPAC branching index `g` is introduced only as an example of a defined branching-sensitive quantity; it is not promoted to a required piping metric.
- No named material-specific branch→property direction is introduced.
- No architecture descriptor is converted into a quality ranking or piping acceptance rule.
