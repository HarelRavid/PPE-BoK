# Chapter 016 — Investigation 006 Authoring Review

**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Investigation:** 6 — How Do Short-Chain and Long-Chain Branching Differ?  
**Disposition:** **PASS**

## 1. Review scope

Reviewed the controlled Investigation 6 authoring candidate against CDB-016, the Technical Outline, active Standards/Evidence Plan, current IUPAC branch terminology and the official ISO 18177:2025 publication page.

## 2. SCB / LCB terminology gate

**PASS.**

The candidate correctly preserves the IUPAC distinction that:

- an oligomeric branch may be termed a short-chain branch;
- a polymeric branch may be termed a long-chain branch.

It deliberately avoids inventing one universal branch-length or carbon-count cut-off for all polymers and methods.

## 3. ISO 18177:2025 scope gate

**PASS.**

Official ISO scope checked on 2026-08-16 confirms:

- ISO 18177:2025, Edition 1, published 2025-05;
- method: successive self-nucleation and annealing (SSA) using conventional, high-performance or fast DSC;
- purpose: estimation of short-chain branching distribution of semicrystalline ethylene/1-olefin copolymers;
- applicable to raw materials and products within that material family;
- quantitative calculation of SCB content, degree of crystallinity and SCB distribution is stated for ethylene/1-butene, ethylene/1-hexene and ethylene/1-octene copolymers.

The candidate keeps these limits attached and does not generalize the method to PP or arbitrary thermoplastics.

## 4. Directness / inference boundary

**PASS.**

The text correctly describes SSA-DSC as a thermal estimation/fractionation route and not as direct imaging/counting of molecular branches.

It preserves the evidence chain:

`thermal response under method → method-defined SCB estimate`

and blocks direct inference to complete branch topology, LCB or piping performance.

## 5. Distribution-object boundary

**PASS.**

The candidate correctly distinguishes:

- molar-mass distribution;
- SCB/comonomer distribution;
- LCB topology distribution;
- process provenance.

It requires the object of the word `distribution` to be stated explicitly.

## 6. LCB evidence boundary

**PASS.**

LCB is not inferred from ISO 18177, density or MFR. The candidate correctly routes LCB claims to topology-sensitive evidence and requires direct versus inferred status to remain visible.

## 7. Assets

**PASS.**

- `FIG-016-003` has a final topology-only specification;
- `FIG-016-004` demonstrates equal average branch amount versus different distribution without implying a commercial PE architecture;
- `TAB-016-003` separates descriptor, directness and evidence limits.

## 8. Chapter-boundary review

**PASS.**

Detailed DSC procedure remains deferred to Part V; morphology/property implications remain downstream and gated.

## 9. Decision

**PASS. Investigation 7 authorized.**

Investigation 7 shall open the IUPAC crosslink/network terminology gate and the ISO 10147:2011 PE-X gel-content scope gate. Gel content must remain a method-specific network/insoluble-fraction indicator and must not be promoted to a complete crosslink-density or network-topology measurement.
