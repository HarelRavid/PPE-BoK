# Chapter 016 — Investigation 002 Authoring Review

**Date:** 2026-08-16  
**Investigation:** 2 — What Are Chain Length, Degree of Polymerization and Molar Mass?  
**Disposition:** **PASS**

## 1. Review scope

Reviewed the canonical Investigation 2 against CDB-016, the Chapter 016 Technical Outline, the active Standards/Evidence Plan and current IUPAC terminology.

## 2. Terminology gate

**PASS.**

The investigation correctly separates:

- informal `chain length` from a controlled count or measured quantity;
- degree of polymerization as a dimensionless count-based descriptor;
- molar mass `M` as a dimensional quantity with mass-per-amount units;
- relative molecular mass `M_r` as a dimensionless ratio;
- historical/industry `molecular weight` terminology from the preferred controlled dimensional term `molar mass`;
- apparent molar mass from a fully corrected/method-independent architecture claim.

Current terminology basis checked against IUPAC Gold Book 5th ed., online v5.0.0 and the IUPAC Recommendations 2014 source set already registered as S016-001/S016-002.

## 3. Quantitative boundary

**PASS.**

Investigation 2 introduces only a conceptual bookkeeping relation between repeat-derived-unit count and chain molar mass. It does not promote that relation to a general retained equation or use it for product/design inference.

The manuscript correctly states that composition, end groups, branching/graft architecture and population averaging can invalidate a simplistic one-to-one DP↔molar-mass conversion.

## 4. Copolymer boundary

**PASS.**

The investigation correctly warns that degree-of-polymerization and molar-mass distributions must remain conceptually distinct when chain composition is non-uniform. It does not generalize the homopolymer proportionality shortcut to arbitrary copolymers.

## 5. Measurement boundary

**PASS.**

The apparent-molar-mass discussion is used only to prepare the SEC/GPC measurement discussion in Investigation 4. No SEC calibration claim is converted into an absolute universal rule.

## 6. Chapter-boundary check

**PASS.**

No morphology, rheology, fracture, SCG, joining or pressure-design treatment is pulled forward from Chapters 017–020 or later Parts.

## 7. Decision

**PASS. Investigation 3 authorized.**

Investigation 3 shall open the controlled equation gate for `M_n`, `M_m/M_w`, molar-mass distribution and `Đ_M`, including a worked simplified population example. Every retained equation must define weighting basis, units, assumptions and what the quantity does not establish about piping performance.
