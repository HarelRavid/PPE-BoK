## 1. Controlling terminology

PASS.

Current Chapter 016 terminology is based on the active IUPAC path recorded as S016-001 through S016-005, including current Gold Book usage for:

- degree of polymerization;
- molar mass / molar-mass averages;
- relative molecular mass;
- dispersity;

## 2. ISO lifecycle / scope validation

### ISO 472:2013 + Amd 1:2018

PASS as vocabulary cross-check with lifecycle hold.

- ISO 472:2013 remains Published but at stage 90.92 — to be revised;
- Amendment 1:2018 remains Published;
- replacement-series work is under development.

Therefore ISO 472 is not used as a stable design authority and requires publication-time lifecycle recheck.

### ISO 16014-1 through -5:2019

PASS for method-scope use.

Official ISO public records confirm the 2019 family remains Published/Confirmed.

Controlled distinctions retained:

- Part 1 — general SEC principles;
- Part 2 — universal calibration, relative method;
- Part 3 — low-temperature organic-eluent route below 60 °C, relative;
- Part 4 — high-temperature organic-eluent route 60–220 °C, relative;
- Part 5 — SEC-LS absolute-molecular-weight route within stated method scope.

No method-equivalence or assumption-free claim is made.

### ISO 18177:2025

PASS within exact scope.

Used only for SSA-DSC estimation of SCB distribution in semicrystalline ethylene/1-olefin copolymers, with quantitative material systems kept within the public ISO scope.

Not used for LCB, universal PE branching or pipe-performance qualification.

### ISO 10147:2011

PASS.

Official ISO status remains Published / Confirmed 90.93.

Used only for PE-X gel-content-by-solvent-extraction assessment of degree of crosslinking. The chapter explicitly blocks `gel content = complete crosslink density/topology`.

### ISO 1133-1:2022

PASS.

Official ISO scope confirms MFR/MVR under specified temperature/load conditions and notes the method is used primarily in quality control; processing correlation is not guaranteed.

The chapter correctly blocks MFR/MVR as direct MMD/branch-topology measurements.

### ISO 1133-2:2011

PASS with lifecycle recheck hold.

The official ISO page identifies the method for materials sensitive to time-temperature history and/or moisture and states the version remains current after review activity. Publication-time status shall be rechecked.
