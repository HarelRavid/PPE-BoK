## 2. IUPAC terminology

Official ISO status at recheck:

- Published;
- Edition 3;
- stage `90.93 — International Standard confirmed`.

## 4. ISO 18177:2025

Official ISO status at recheck:

- Published;
- Edition 1;
- publication date 2025-05;
- stage `60.60 — International Standard published`.

The public scope confirms successive self-nucleation and annealing (SSA) with DSC for estimation of short-chain-branching distribution in semicrystalline ethylene/1-olefin copolymers.

The public scope specifically states quantitative calculation of SCB content, degree of crystallinity and SCB distribution for ethylene/1-butene, ethylene/1-hexene and ethylene/1-octene copolymers.

Chapter 016 retains this scope boundary.

## 5. ISO 10147:2011

Official ISO status at recheck:

- Published;
- Edition 3;
- stage `90.93 — International Standard confirmed`.

The public scope confirms assessment/estimation of degree of crosslinking in PE-X pipes and fittings by determination of gel content through solvent extraction.

Chapter 016 correctly prevents gel content from being treated as a complete crosslink-density or network-topology measurement.
