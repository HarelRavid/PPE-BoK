## Known limitations

1. This chapter provides conceptual foundations and intentionally avoids material-specific allowable values.
2. Chemical-resistance selection methods are deferred to a dedicated chapter.
3. Detailed rheological models and constitutive equations are deferred to mechanical-design chapters.
4. PE100-RC requirements vary by governing product-standard edition and region; project specifications must cite the exact standard and edition.
5. The ISO/FDIS 15494 status is temporally unstable and must be rechecked before release.
