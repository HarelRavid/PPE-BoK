**Status:** Controlled central register — repository-documented editions/status; authoritative Standards Validation required before publication

This register is the central navigation index for standards already used or identified across PPE-BoK. It does not reproduce protected standards text and does not replace direct consultation of authoritative sources.

**Important distinction:** an edition/status shown here records what is currently documented in the repository source registers. It is **not** a certification that the edition remains current on the publication date. Current edition, scope, clause/table references, numerical values and normative interpretations must be verified during the applicable Standards Validation gate.

## Standards watch

### ISO 15494 replacement edition

Chapter 009–011 source records note that a replacement / Edition 3 development item was in progress during research. That item is retained as a **standards-watch trigger only**.

Do not treat a draft/FDIS item as the published governing edition unless authoritative Standards Validation confirms its publication status and applicability at the time of use.

## Cross-framework items not yet registered as specific standards

Chapters 010–012 identify future interfaces with ASTM, PPI, EN, DVS, AWWA, ASME and other national/application frameworks. These are **scope reminders**, not specific standards-register entries until an exact document is selected and documented for chapter use.

The final book must avoid silently mixing ISO terminology (for example MRS/design coefficient/SDR) with ASTM/PPI terminology (for example HDB/HDS/DR) without an explicit cross-framework explanation.

## Register rules

1. `references/Standards-Register.md` is the single active central standards index. Retired standards-index material under `archive/` is non-governing.
2. A chapter may identify a standard during authoring, but publication requires completion of the dedicated Standards Validation gate against the authoritative source.
3. Exact titles, editions, scope, clause numbers, numerical values, coefficients, tables and normative interpretations are not considered publication-validated merely because they appear in this register.
4. If a standard edition/status changes, all chapters listed in `Chapters currently using / mapping it` shall be reviewed for impact.
5. Standards-derived requirements take precedence over secondary summaries or generated text.
6. Chapter-specific `references.md` files remain the detailed evidence record and shall be synchronized with this central index when standards are added, removed or materially re-scoped.
7. Do not create a register row for a generic framework name alone when no exact standard/document has been selected.
