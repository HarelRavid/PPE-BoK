## Register rules

1. Exact titles, editions, clause numbers, numerical values and normative interpretations are not considered validated until checked directly against authoritative standards.
2. A chapter may cite the standard identifier during authoring, but publication requires completion of the dedicated Standards Validation gate.
3. If a standard edition changes, all chapters listed in `Chapters using it` shall be reviewed for impact.
4. Standards-derived requirements take precedence over secondary summaries or generated text.
