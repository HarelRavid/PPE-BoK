## Boundary confirmation

Chapter 006 is now the canonical chapter for technical construction of the service/design envelope:

`operating/life-cycle states + condition ranges + credible combinations + time + local conditions → discrete envelope cases → downstream engineering checks`

Chapter 005 remains the canonical chapter for Design Basis integration, ownership, approval, readiness, configuration control, and change management.

Chapter 007 remains the canonical chapter for engineering risk and uncertainty treatment.

Detailed code-specific load combinations, equations, and acceptance criteria remain outside Chapter 006 and belong to the relevant downstream specialist design methods.
