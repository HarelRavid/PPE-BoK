## 2. Controlled holds intentionally carried forward

The following remain open and are explicitly deferred to a later full-text standards closure gate:

1. ISO 12162:2009 — exact MRS definition, classification/rounding series, PE100 mapping, design coefficient `C`, design-stress notation and rounding.
2. ISO 9080:2012 — exact statistical lower-bound terminology/notation, data requirements, extrapolation limits and branch/knee rules.
3. ISO 4427 current published path — exact PFA/PN/SDR relationships, pressure tables, temperature clauses and mandatory marking requirements.
4. ISO 4437 current published path — exact MOP calculation, RCP constraints, derating details, marking and component/joint limits.
5. PE100-RC — exact product-standard acceptance route, qualification method(s), thresholds and pressure-design implications, if any.
6. SCG qualification — exact acceptance/equivalence rules between applicable methods where asserted by the governing product/application specification.

These holds are not chapter-architecture defects. They are controlled source-access / clause-level verification requirements.
