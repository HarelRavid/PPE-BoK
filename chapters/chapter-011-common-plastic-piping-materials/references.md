# Chapter 11 — References and Evidence Map

## Primary standards

1. **ISO 15494:2015** — *Plastics piping systems for industrial applications — Polybutene (PB), polyethylene (PE), polyethylene of raised temperature resistance (PE-RT), crosslinked polyethylene (PE-X), polypropylene (PP) — Metric series for specifications for components and the system.*
   - Primary scope source for industrial PE and PP systems.
   - ISO lists Amendment 1:2020 as published and indicates that a replacement edition is under development.

2. **ISO 15493:2003** — *Plastics piping systems for industrial applications — Acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene (ABS), unplasticized poly(vinyl chloride) (PVC-U) and chlorinated poly(vinyl chloride) (PVC-C) — Specifications for components and the system — Metric series.*
   - Primary scope source for industrial ABS, PVC-U, and PVC-C systems.
   - Confirmed by ISO in 2025; Amendment 1:2016 and Corrigendum 1:2004 should be checked with the base standard.

3. **ISO 10931:2005** — *Plastics piping systems for industrial applications — Poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF) — Specifications for components and the system.*
   - Primary scope source for industrial PVDF systems.
   - Confirmed by ISO in 2025; Amendment 1:2015 applies.

4. **ISO/TR 10358:2021** — *Plastics pipes and fittings — Combined chemical-resistance classification table.*
   - Screening reference for chemical resistance across common thermoplastic families.
   - Must not be treated as a pressure-rating or service-life approval.

5. **ISO 9080:2012** — *Plastics piping and ducting systems — Determination of the long-term hydrostatic strength of thermoplastics materials in pipe form by extrapolation.*
   - Long-term hydrostatic-strength methodology.

6. **ISO 12162:2009** — *Thermoplastics materials for pipes and fittings for pressure applications — Classification, designation and design coefficient.*
   - Material classification and MRS framework.

7. **ISO 14692 series** — *Petroleum and natural gas industries — Glass-reinforced plastics (GRP) piping.*
   - Relevant framework for composite piping where within scope.

## Industry and technical sources

- **PE100+ Association** — quality requirements and listed-material framework.
  - Used only to explain the meaning of PE100+.
  - Not treated as an ISO material class.

- **Plastics Pipe Institute (PPI), Handbook of Polyethylene Pipe**.
  - Engineering background for PE behaviour, joining, installation, and long-term performance.

- **Brydson, Plastics Materials**.
  - Polymer-family structure and engineering characteristics.

- **Osswald and Menges, Materials Science of Polymers for Engineers**.
  - Structure-property relationships, viscoelasticity, and processing effects.

- Manufacturer technical manuals from multiple established system suppliers may be used during the next review for product-specific joining and service limitations. No single manufacturer's data should be generalized to the entire material family.

## Evidence limitations

- The chapter intentionally avoids universal numerical temperature limits, pressure limits, support spacings, and chemical ratings because these depend on grade, dimensions, standard, service duration, and manufacturer system data.
- PTFE, PFA, and ECTFE discussion is qualitative. Detailed material-specific standards and supplier data are required before those sections can be considered final.
- The current status of ISO 15494 must be checked immediately before publication because ISO indicates a replacement edition is in development.
- ASME, ASTM, DVS, EN, AWWA, and application-specific standards will be mapped in later material-family and joining chapters.
