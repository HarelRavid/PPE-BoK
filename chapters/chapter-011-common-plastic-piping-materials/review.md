## Known limitations

1. The chapter is intentionally qualitative and should not be used to select a material without Chapter 10 and product-specific data.
2. Statements about chemical resistance are family-level descriptions, not approvals for any process chemical.
3. The specialty-fluoropolymer section requires deeper standards mapping in a later dedicated chapter or appendix.
4. Fire performance, electrostatic behaviour, conductive compounds, high-purity classifications, and regulatory approvals require application-specific treatment later in the book.
5. Composite piping is introduced only to prevent incorrect equivalence with thermoplastics; detailed composite design is outside this chapter.
