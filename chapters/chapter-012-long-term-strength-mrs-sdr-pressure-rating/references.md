# Chapter 12 References

## Primary standards

1. **ISO 9080:2012** — *Plastics piping and ducting systems — Determination of the long-term hydrostatic strength of thermoplastics materials in pipe form by extrapolation.* Current edition confirmed by ISO in 2023.
2. **ISO 12162:2009** — *Thermoplastics materials for pipes and fittings for pressure applications — Classification, designation and design coefficient.* Current edition confirmed by ISO in 2021.
3. **ISO 4427 series** — *Plastics piping systems for water supply, and for drainage and sewerage under pressure — Polyethylene (PE).* Use the current applicable part and edition.
4. **ISO 4437 series** — *Plastics piping systems for the supply of gaseous fuels — Polyethylene (PE).* Use the current applicable part and edition.
5. **ISO 15494** — *Plastics piping systems for industrial applications — Polybutene (PB), polyethylene (PE), polyethylene of raised temperature resistance (PE-RT), crosslinked polyethylene (PE-X), polypropylene (PP) and poly(vinylidene fluoride) (PVDF) — Metric series for specifications for components and the system.* Verify the current project-applicable edition.
6. **ISO 17456:2006** — *Plastics piping systems — Multilayer pipes — Determination of long-term strength.* Relevant where multilayer construction is discussed.

## Industry technical sources

7. **PE100+ Association** — *What is SDR pipe and pressure rating?* Technical guidance explaining SDR and the relationship between MRS, design coefficient, SDR and MOP. Last update recorded by the source: 22 January 2024.
8. **PE100+ Association** — Quality Materials List and PE100+ quality-association requirements. Use to distinguish PE100+ listing from MRS classification.
9. **Plastics Pipe Institute (PPI)** — *Handbook of Polyethylene Pipe*, current available edition. Use for North American terminology, pressure design basis concepts, DR relationships, surge and application-specific design guidance. Do not merge ASTM/PPI terminology with ISO terminology without identifying the framework.

## Evidence notes

- ISO 9080 is the primary source for the statistical extrapolation method. This chapter does not reproduce the protected standard procedure.
- ISO 12162 is the primary source for material classification, designation, and design-stress calculation in the ISO framework.
- Product standards determine application-specific coefficients, pressure-temperature relationships, component requirements, and permitted use.
- PE100+ Association material is an industry source, not an ISO material-strength classification standard.
- PPI/ASTM concepts such as HDB, HDS and DR belong to a related but not identical design framework. A later chapter should compare ISO and ASTM/PPI terminology explicitly rather than treating the terms as synonyms.
