# Chapter 10 — References and Evidence Map

## Normative and primary technical sources

1. **ISO/TR 10358:2021** — *Plastics pipes and fittings for industrial applications — Collection of data on combined chemical-resistance.*
   - Use: preliminary chemical-resistance screening across common thermoplastic piping materials, fluids, concentrations, and temperatures.
   - Limitation: collected resistance data do not independently establish pressure rating, component-system suitability, joint performance, or fitness for a specific proprietary mixture.
   - Official record: https://www.iso.org/standard/77005.html

2. **ISO 15494:2015**, including **ISO 15494:2015/Amd 1:2020** — *Plastics piping systems for industrial applications — PB, PE, PE-RT, PE-X and PP — Metric series for specifications for components and the system.*
   - Use: industrial system and component requirements for covered material families.
   - Current-status note: the published 2015 edition and 2020 amendment should be checked again before final publication because ISO reports a replacement edition under development.
   - Official records: https://www.iso.org/standard/55627.html and https://www.iso.org/standard/78114.html

3. **ISO 9080:2012** — *Plastics piping and ducting systems — Determination of the long-term hydrostatic strength of thermoplastics materials in pipe form by extrapolation.*
   - Use: basis for explaining long-term hydrostatic-strength prediction from pipe test data.
   - Official record: https://www.iso.org/standard/43860.html

4. **ISO 12162:2009** — *Thermoplastics materials for pipes and fittings for pressure applications — Classification, designation and design coefficient.*
   - Use: material classification, designation, minimum required strength, and design-coefficient framework.
   - Verify edition status before final publication.

5. **Relevant product and application standards**
   - ISO 4427 series for PE water-supply and related pressure applications.
   - ISO 4437 series for PE gaseous-fuel systems.
   - Applicable ASTM, EN, DVS, AWWA, PPI, ASME, national, client, and regulatory documents depending on service and jurisdiction.
   - Use: system-specific product, joining, design, installation, and test requirements.

## Sources intentionally not used as sole authority

- Generic online chemical-resistance charts without stated compound, temperature, concentration, test method, or revision.
- Distributor summaries that reproduce manufacturer data without context.
- Marketing claims not linked to a standard, test method, or documented field evidence.
- Material-family names used without confirmation of the actual pipe compound and product-system approval.
