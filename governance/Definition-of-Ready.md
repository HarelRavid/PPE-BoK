# PPE-BoK Definition of Ready

**Version:** 1.0

A chapter is ready for Engineering Development only when all of the following are defined and approved:

- Chapter Design Brief (CDB)
- chapter purpose and scope
- primary reader outcomes
- Design Basis variables
- applicable standards list / standards path
- Investigation structure / technical outline
- required engineering assets
- known exclusions and cross-references
- success / acceptance criteria

If one of these is materially undefined, the chapter remains in planning and shall not enter full redevelopment.
