# PPE-BoK Definition of Done

**Version:** 1.0

A chapter is complete only when all required gates are closed:

- approved CDB satisfied;
- engineering narrative complete;
- required engineering assets complete or approved for Publishing when visuals are production placeholders;
- equations, units and numerical examples independently checked;
- Technical Review complete;
- Standards Validation complete against authoritative source documents after authoring;
- evidence / objectivity review complete;
- editorial and style review complete;
- chapter passes the Desk Test;
- joint author review complete;
- explicit author approval received;
- approved revision committed and merged to `main`;
- chapter version and repository history updated.

A chapter with pending standards validation, unresolved calculations, open technical comments or unapproved content is not baselined.
